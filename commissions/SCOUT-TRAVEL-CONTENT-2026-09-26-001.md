## Objective
Determine whether the Founder's existing original travel-media library and lived destination experience can support a sustainable Facebook-led travel-content property with multiple revenue lanes, including Facebook Content Monetization and the Founder's existing Viator affiliate capability.

## Geographic source lanes
Puerto Rico; Dominican Republic; Colombia; New York City; Florida; Colorado; Wyoming.

## Originating lead
A Reddit discussion claimed that travel/entertainment Facebook pages posting high-frequency AI-assisted text-and-image content could reach Facebook Content Monetization and generate recurring monthly revenue. These anecdotal claims are **UNVERIFIED LEADS**, not facts or expected revenue.

Scout must independently verify current platform rules and distinguish:
- primary-source platform policy,
- third-party observation,
- anecdotal earnings claims,
- Scout analysis/inference.

## Founder-owned advantage to evaluate
The Founder reports an existing library of original personal travel images across the geographic lanes above. The strategy should prioritize authentic original media and lived context rather than mass-produced synthetic imagery.

The Founder also has an existing Viator affiliate capability. Scout should evaluate how commercial-intent travel posts can use appropriate affiliate offers without turning every post into an advertisement.

## Required investigation
Scout must return evidence for:
1. Current Meta/Facebook Content Monetization eligibility and originality requirements.
2. Current treatment of AI-assisted versus unoriginal/reused content.
3. Whether 8–10 Facebook posts per day is sensible as a test cadence; do not assume frequency itself causes monetization.
4. Destination/niche opportunity across the seven source lanes.
5. Whether one umbrella travel property or multiple destination-specific pages is better for an initial pilot.
6. A content taxonomy: reach, engagement, story/authority, travel utility, and commercial-intent posts.
7. Viator integration points and disclosure requirements.
8. A selective Trending Edit cross-post strategy; do not blindly duplicate the travel feed.
9. A measurement plan covering reach, engagement, follows/unfollows, link clicks, affiliate conversion signals, monetization status, production time, and content reuse.
10. Risks: platform policy, spam signals, repetitive content, copyright/ownership, disclosure, audience mismatch, and dependence on any single revenue stream.
